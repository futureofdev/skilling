# Skilling Basics

This phase takes you from "I've never seen this format" to having authored and validated a course of your own.

We work outside-in: first the shape of a whole course, then the shape of a single lesson, then what a tutor actually does with them. By the end you'll be able to read any Skilling course and know exactly what a tutor will do with it — which is the useful thing, because you'll be writing courses for tutors you've never met.

Nothing here requires you to install anything until the last lesson's homework, where you'll validate your own course with the `skilling` CLI.
