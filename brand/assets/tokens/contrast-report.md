# Contrast report

Skilling brand kit v2.0. Generated by `brand/build.py` — do not hand-edit.

Computed with the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula. The implementation is validated on every run against three known reference pairs: `#767676` on white = 4.54:1, black on white = 21.00:1, and `#0000FF` on white = 8.59:1.

`fail` is below 3:1. `AA-large` is 3:1–4.5:1, permitted only for text at 24px+, or 18.66px+ bold, or for non-text elements.

## The mark is not a contrast problem

The Cut's legibility constraint is figure–ground, not contrast. White on Ink measures 17.74:1 — an excellent ratio — and the mark is still unreadable, because at 80.1% coverage the channels are the counters, and inverting them turns the counters into strokes. No ratio can tell you that; it was found by rendering the mark and looking at it.
